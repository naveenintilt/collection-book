Privacy Policy

Collection Book · Last updated 20 October 2026

Collection Book is a personal-use Android app for recording loan collections. This policy explains what the app stores and how it uses Google account data.

Data the app stores

Customer names, loan amounts, repayment schedules and payment entries are entered by the person using the app. This data is stored on that person's own phone. It is not sent to any server operated by the developer, and it is not sold, rented or shared with any third party.

Google account data

Connecting a Google account is optional and is used for one purpose: backing up the app's own data to that account's Google Drive.

  • The app creates a folder named Collection Book Backups and writes backup files into it.
  • The app only accesses files it created itself. It does not read, modify or delete any other file in your Drive.
  • Backups stay in your Drive. The developer has no access to them.
  • No Google account data is used for advertising, profiling, or sold to anyone.

Collection Book's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deleting data

  • Disconnect Google Drive at any time from the app's Settings screen, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete backups by deleting the Collection Book Backups folder in your Drive.
  • Delete app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
