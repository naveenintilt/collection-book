Collection Book

A small Android app for keeping track of loan collections.

What it does

  • Records customers, loan amounts and repayment schedules.
  • Tracks daily and weekly collection entries.
  • Produces a receipt for each payment that can be shared.
  • Backs the collection book up to the owner's own Google Drive.

Who it is for

Collection Book is a private, personal-use app. It is not distributed on the Google Play Store and is installed directly by its owner.

Google Drive backup

If you connect a Google account, the app creates a folder named Collection Book Backups in that account's Drive and writes a backup copy of the app's own data into it. It does not read, change or delete any other file in your Drive.
